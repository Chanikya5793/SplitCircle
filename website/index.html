Private by architecture

Your group is not our content library.

ManaSplit uses the cloud to move messages, not to keep a permanent copy. Message history and call history stay on your devices. Persistent servers hold the account, group, and expense records needed to keep a shared ledger working.

Questions, answered

The things people ask before joining a circle.

?
Does ManaSplit move money?

No. ManaSplit records expenses and calculates balances. Payments happen outside the app.

Where are messages stored?

Message history is local-first on your devices. Cloud transit queues are designed to expire after delivery.

Can I use it without a perfect connection?

Yes. ManaSplit can queue supported work offline and includes nearby delivery for trusted devices.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can initiate full account deletion from the app. We also publish a step-by-step deletion guide.

Does ManaSplit show ads or track me?

No. ManaSplit does not show ads, sell personal information, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
