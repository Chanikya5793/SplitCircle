Launch, hold, and learn

Launch foundation

One commercial system

  • One Apple subscription group
  • Free and paid included-use pools
  • Non-expiring purchased credits
  • Server verification and an append-only ledger
  • Owner access through Sandbox, TestFlight, and an audited test role
Hold

Do not ship yet

  • Individual permanent products for every split mode
  • Roulette or Double Wheel paid with credits
  • Wallet-to-wallet transfers, re-gifting, trading, or cash-out
  • Five paid choices on one screen
  • Any product ID before names and boundaries are stable
Learn first

Shadow measurement

  • Mode-level repeat usage
  • Local versus provider execution route
  • Real cost and latency distributions
  • Exhaustion behavior and willingness to pay
  • Bundle versus individual-mode preference

The credit system

Included uses

Resetting access

  • Comes from Free or the active subscription
  • Resets by its stated window
  • Does not roll over or transfer
  • Is not described as wallet currency
Purchased Mana Credits

Durable consumables

  • Purchased through StoreKit
  • Never expire
  • Remain after subscription cancellation
  • Work without a subscription for every advertised eligible use

Permanent

Owned local capability wins.

Plan use

Consume the active plan allowance.

Promotion

Use expiring free lots first.

Confirm

Show exact credit cost and balance.

Reserve

Commit only after useful success.

A provider error, cancellation, duplicate retry, or on-device fallback costs zero credits. Roulette, Double Wheel, and Karma stay outside the credit economy during the policy hold.

Friend credits: one-way gifts only, later

Launch

No gifting

First prove ordinary purchase, ledger, refund, account recovery, and support operations.

Pilot later

Buy for a named friend

Bind the recipient before StoreKit confirmation. Grant a non-expiring, non-exchangeable lot that cannot be re-gifted.

Hold

No open transfer economy

No wallet transfers, trade, resale, pool, cash-out, debt settlement, gift cards, crypto, or physical-goods conversion.

Refunds go to the original purchaser. Lot provenance must identify unused gift units. Transferability requires specialist legal, fraud, geography, and App Review work before engineering.

Owner access and customer support

Xcode StoreKit

Fast local tests for purchase, renewal, failure, interruption, expiration, and refund before products exist.

Sandbox and TestFlight

End-to-end Apple purchase infrastructure with no tester charge and a ledger isolated from production.

Audited internal-test role

Exact owner UID, visible badge, server-issued grant, environment scoping, strong authentication, and complete bypass audit.

Remote support calls cannot change the caller's own test access. Owner and administrator self-access changes use the audited local npm run internal-test:access operator command. Free StoreKit testing does not make LiveKit, Firebase, or third-party provider work free. Use fixtures, local models, and provider sandboxes for unlimited repetition; retain hard provider-safety budgets even for the owner account.

Support console may expose

  • Exact-user entitlements and included-use state
  • Purchased, promotional, and gifted credit lots
  • Reservation, commit, release, refund, and reversal history
  • Sanitized device/provider health
  • Narrow, reason-bound corrective actions

Support console must not expose

  • Message or call content
  • Encryption keys or backup contents
  • Receipt contents or monitored identity plaintext
  • Unrestricted database browsing
  • User impersonation or silent ledger edits

Correctness gates before charging anyone

Advanced split integrity

  • Align weightedRoulette, Double Wheel, scrooge, and Karma names with behavior
  • Preserve receipt tax/tip split metadata through edit and re-save
  • Persist only fields required by the chosen mode
  • Use and test one randomness contract

Every entry path

  • Gate UI, receipt, deep link, Siri, native, and server paths
  • Count only the final successful expense save
  • Use signed offline allowance leases for local-first creation
  • Never reject an already valid offline financial record on reconnect

Rollout sequence

  1. Product integrity: fix naming, persistence, data minimization, randomness, headless paths, and split-math tests.
  2. Shadow mode: measure four weeks with no blocking, price, or paywall.
  3. Purchase foundation: verify StoreKit, restore, notifications, refunds, non-expiring ledger, and sandbox separation.
  4. Small launch: Plus, Pro, Max, two small credit packs, and one low-risk accelerator.
  5. Evidence expansion: permanent bundles, contextual tiers, and direct friend gifts only after their decision gates pass.
