Quick checks

Update iOS and ManaSplit.
Use the latest version available to you in TestFlight or the App Store.
Check the service and permissions.
Confirm Wi-Fi or cellular data, notifications, camera, microphone, photos, and local-network access for the feature.
Restart the failed flow.
Close the sheet or conversation, reopen it, and try once more. Avoid repeated retries when an upload or payment-like ledger change is still processing.
Send a precise support note.
Include what you expected, what happened, the time, device model, iOS version, and app version. Redact names, balances, messages, and tokens.
