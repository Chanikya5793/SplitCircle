Account control

Delete your account, not just the app.

Deleting the app from your iPhone does not delete your ManaSplit account. Use the in-app flow below to permanently delete the account and associated server data.

Delete in ManaSplit

Open ManaSplit and select Settings.
Open your Account or account-management settings.
Choose Delete account and review what will be removed.
If you own a group, transfer ownership or delete the group when the app asks. This prevents shared records from becoming ownerless.
Confirm deletion and complete any recent-sign-in check shown by your identity provider.
This action is permanent. Your sign-in account, profile, device registrations, Security Center records, and personal server data are removed. Shared expense history may be adjusted or retained for other group members when necessary to preserve their records or meet legal obligations. Device-local content is removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app.

Cannot access the app?

Email privacy@manasplit.com from the address associated with your account. Include only the request and the sign-in method you used. Do not send a password, Apple authorization code, backup passphrase, or identity document unless we specifically explain a secure verification process.

We will verify account ownership and explain any lawful retention. Manual requests are completed within the time required by applicable law.

Sign in with Apple

Deleting your ManaSplit account also triggers revocation steps for supported Sign in with Apple credentials. You can separately review apps using your Apple Account in iOS Settings.
