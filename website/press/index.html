Press kit

ManaSplit, correctly described.

Approved product language and brand assets for editors, reviewers, event organizers, and partners.

One line

Boilerplate

ManaSplit is a local-first iPhone app that brings shared expenses, private group messaging, and audio and video calls into one group.

Category

What it is

Personal finance and private communication software for friends, roommates, families, trips, and recurring shared households.

Position

Why it exists

Because the money is part of the conversation, and a group should not need three apps to coordinate one plan.

Name and capitalization

Use ManaSplit, with a capital M and S and no space. The company and product name are the same for public copy. “SplitCircle” is an internal technical identifier and should not appear in consumer-facing coverage.

Approved short description

ManaSplit keeps the ledger and the group together. People can split expenses in flexible ways, send private messages and media, and start audio or video calls from the group that already contains the plan. Message and call history are local-first, while shared expense records synchronize for the group.

Logo downloads

Primary SVG Reversed SVG App icon PNG

Keep clear space around the mark. Do not redraw, rotate, skew, recolor, add effects, or separate the four petals and dots. Use the reversed mark on deep plum.

Product claims

  • Say “local-first,” not “offline-only.”
  • Say supported messages are end-to-end encrypted. Do not claim calls are end-to-end encrypted unless the published build and configuration have been independently verified for that mode.
  • Do not describe ManaSplit as a bank, wallet, money transmitter, payment service, or financial adviser.
  • Do not say that AI makes financial decisions. Deterministic calculations own totals; AI explains and helps navigate.
